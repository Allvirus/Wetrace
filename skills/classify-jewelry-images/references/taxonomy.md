# Approved Taxonomy

Product category is single-select:

- `plain_chain`: 素链
- `pendant`: 吊坠
- `bangle`: 手镯
- `bead_bracelet`: 手串
- `ring`: 戒指
- `bracelet`: 手链
- `earring`: 耳饰
- `collar`: 项圈
- `necklace_set`: 套链
- `accessory`: 配件

Processes are multi-select:

- `plain_gold`: 素金
- `ancient_craft`: 古法
- `x5g`: x5G
- `x3d`: x3D
- `x5d`: x5D
- `wedding`: 婚庆
- `platinum`: 铂金
- `white_silver`: 白银
